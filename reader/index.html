FTFC
Full Timeframe Continuity: trade the direction all timeframes agree on.
HH · HL
Higher highs + higher lows = uptrend (market structure). Look for longs on pullbacks.
LH · LL
Lower highs + lower lows = downtrend. Look for shorts on rallies.
BOS
Break of structure: price breaks the last swing in the trend's direction. Trend continuing.
CHoCH
Change of character: first break against the trend. Early warning it may flip.
Resistance / Support
Price structure: levels where price stalled before. Put targets just before them, stops beyond them.
