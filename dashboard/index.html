Strat Dashboard

One paste → one trade card. Same engine as Strat Chart Reader, Trade Risk Check and Strat Command.

How to use:
  1. In Strat Chart Reader, tap Copy Strat prompt, paste it into Claude with your chart screenshot.
  2. Paste Claude's whole answer below (or tap Use it here when another app offers its last analysis).
  3. Read the card. The apps can't see your chart: prices come only from the analysis or your inputs.

For review only — no live data, no orders placed.
