On-device chart reader

  1. Drag a box around the last 5–10 candles. Leave out the volume bars and the price labels on the right edge.
  2. Click a price gridline (level with a number on the right price axis), then type that number in "Price at line 1".
  3. Click a second gridline and type its number in "Price at line 2".
  4. Press Analyze chart (or Deep read for thin or faint candles).

Works with green/teal up candles and red down candles (TradingView defaults). The same box is reused for your next screenshot if it's the same size.

          How the engine decides
          • Bar types: 1 = inside, 2U = breaks the prior high only, 2D = breaks the prior low only, 3 = breaks both.
          • Setup bar is the last closed bar. Long trigger = its high + 1 tick. Short trigger = its low − 1 tick. Stop = the other side of that bar (or its midpoint if you pick the tighter stop).
          • Targets are prior bar highs (long) or lows (short) beyond the trigger, nearest first. If there are none, targets are set at 1R and your minimum R.
          • Direction: if your higher timeframes all agree, the engine trades with them. Otherwise it uses the pattern: X-1-2 follows the bar before the inside bar, 1-2-2 and 3-2-2 look for the reversal, 2-2 continues.
          • Grade adds points for a clean pattern, timeframe agreement, reward : risk at or above your minimum, and a size that fits your risk limit. It takes points away for chop (lots of 1s and 3s) and for trading against continuity.
          • Signal: A or B and triggered = GO. A or B and not triggered = set the stop order. C = wait. D or F = stand aside.

          Learning tool, not financial advice. The screenshot read can misjudge prices by a tick or more; confirm every level on your live chart before placing an order, and follow your Apex account rules.
